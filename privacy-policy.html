CorePit - Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

CorePit is a virtual reality game developed by Michael Matrix as "Matrix Designs" ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle information when you play CorePit or visit its website.

The game

We do not operate accounts, advertising, or analytics in CorePit, and we do not intentionally collect player data. The game does not ask you to provide a name, email address, or password to play.

CorePit saves some preferences, such as comfort and control settings, locally on your device so they persist between sessions. We do not receive those settings through the game. You can remove the game's local data using your device's app-data controls or by uninstalling the game, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

Meta Quest and other providers

CorePit runs on Meta Quest. Meta handles data through its headset, operating system, and store under Meta's own privacy policy. That platform activity is separate from information we receive directly. The game uses Unity and Meta software components; we do not claim that their presence alone means player data is sent to us. Their data practices are governed by their own policies.

The website

We do not currently provide accounts, forms, advertising, or our own analytics on the CorePit website. If the site is hosted with GitHub Pages, GitHub says it logs visitors' IP addresses for security. GitHub handles that information under its own privacy statement. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message, and use it to respond to you.

Children

We do not intentionally collect information from children through the game. If a child contacts us by email, a parent or guardian may contact us at the address below about that message.

Privacy and deletion requests

To ask what information we hold about you, or to request deletion of an email you sent us, contact corepitgame@gmail.com. We cannot delete information held independently by Meta, GitHub, or your device; contact those providers for their data controls.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when our data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact corepitgame@gmail.com.
